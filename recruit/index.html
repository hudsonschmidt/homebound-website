Recruiting for Homebound

Thanks for helping people find Homebound. This page explains how your code works and the few rules that come with it.

How it works

You have a short code, like SAM, and a link: api.homeboundapp.com/r/ followed by your code. Both are in the app under Settings, then Refer Friends.

Share either one. When someone new signs up with your code and finishes the 2-minute practice trip, or any other trip, you each get 7 days of Homebound+.

They can type your code on the profile step while they set up the app, or open your link once the app is installed. If they miss it, they can add it under Settings, then Refer Friends, within 72 hours of signing up.

The days you earn add up to a yearly limit. If you already have Homebound+, earned days are saved and start when it ends.

What you'll see

Refer Friends shows how many people opened your link, who joined, and who has finished a trip. You see their first name and last initial.

Joining with your code doesn't make someone your Homebound friend. They can add you as a friend if they want to.

The rules

  • Rewards are Homebound+ days. They have no cash value and can't be transferred.
  • Only new accounts count, and each person can only be brought in once.
  • Don't pay people to sign up, and don't make accounts yourself. A reward for someone who signs up on your phone waits for a review.
  • Be honest about what Homebound does. It tells the people you choose when you aren't back by the time you set. It doesn't replace preparation or emergency services.
  • Share with people you know, or in groups where sharing is welcome. No spam.
  • We can pause or end a recruiter code at any time. Days you've already earned stay yours, unless they came from breaking these rules.
