Testing Find

Find points two people on the same group trip toward each other with an arrow and a distance. Up close, newer iPhones use Ultra Wideband to measure the distance far more precisely than GPS can. These tests check how that holds up in real places, with real phones.

2 iPhones 2 people Full run: about an hour Quick run: about 20 minutes

Mark each test Pass, Fail or Skip and add notes as you go. Your answers save on this phone, and the Send your results section at the bottom puts them together for you.

What you need

  • Two iPhones with Ultra Wideband: iPhone 11 or newer, except iPhone SE, 16e and 17e.
  • The Homebound TestFlight build on both phones. Hudson sends the invite.
  • A test account for each phone, also from Hudson.
  • Two people, one per phone.
  • An open field or park, and a busy indoor spot for one short test.

Set up each phone

  1. Install Homebound from TestFlight and sign in with your test account.
  2. In Homebound, go to Settings → Developer Tools → Server Environment and pick Dev Render. This signs you out. Sign in again with the same account.
  3. Back in Settings → Developer Tools, turn on Find UWB Lab.
  4. In the iPhone's Settings app, open Homebound. Set Location to While Using the App or Always, and turn on Precise Location. Turn on Notifications and Live Activities.
  5. Close the Find My app if it's open. It can borrow the Ultra Wideband radio.
  6. Hudson adds both accounts to a group trip. Accept the invite. The trip has to be started for Find to work.

Start a Find

  1. On one phone, open the trip and tap Find & Meet Up. Under Find someone, tap Find next to your partner.
  2. On the other phone, allow it when asked.
  3. The first time, each phone asks to find and measure the distance to nearby devices. Tap Allow on both. Later, Precise arrow asks for the camera. Allow that too.
  4. The UWB lab panel shows under the arrow. It's how you'll read the numbers in the tests below.

While you test

  • Hold both phones upright, with the backs facing each other.
  • Before each test, type the test's name in the Mark box (for example "Test 5 round 2") and tap Mark. That's how Hudson finds each test in the log.
  • After each test, tap Export log on both phones and save or AirDrop the file. Hudson needs both phones' logs.
  • The log holds distances, angles, timings and battery level. It doesn't hold where you are.

The tests

Same model of iPhone (two 17 Pros, say): do the full run, every test below.

Different models (a 17 Pro and a 16, say): do the quick run, just the tests marked Quick run.

1

Quick check

Full runQuick run5 min

Do

  1. Stand 2 to 3 meters apart.
  2. Start a Find and open it on both phones.

What should happen

  • In the lab panel, Caps me / peer shows PCE / PCE. If either side has a D in it, write that down: that phone can point without the camera.
  • Within about 5 seconds, Phase says ranging and Config says EDM on.
  • The Find screen shows an exact distance with Precise distance under it.
  • If the phone can't point by itself, the circle shows a Precise arrow button.
2

Which way is right

Full runQuick run5 min

Do

  1. Tap Precise arrow and move your phone slowly side to side until the arrow shows up.
  2. Have your partner stand about 3 meters to your right.
  3. Then about 3 meters to your left.
  4. Swap and do it on the other phone.
  5. Quick run: if test 1 showed a D on a phone, also do this on that phone without tapping Precise arrow.

What should happen

  • Partner on your right: the arrow points right, and Angle in the lab panel is a positive number.
  • Partner on your left: the arrow points left, and Angle is negative.

If it's backwards on either phone, stop and tell Hudson before going on. It's a quick fix, and it changes what the other tests mean.

3

The camera arrow

Full run10 min

Do

  1. Tap Precise arrow and time how long it takes for the arrow to appear. Convergence in the lab panel changes to converged at the same moment.
  2. With the arrow up, have your partner walk a full circle around you, about 5 meters out, then stop behind you.
  3. Try it once more somewhere dim.

Write down

  • How many seconds until the first arrow.
  • Whether the arrow keeps up as they walk around you, and what it does when they're behind you.
  • Any tips that show at the top of the camera, like "Find more light".
  • If the camera picture goes black but the arrow still works, write that down. That's a backup mode, not a failure. If it says "Precise arrow couldn't start the camera", tap Try again once, and export the log either way.
4

Found each other

Full run5 min

Do

  1. Start about 10 meters apart.
  2. Walk toward each other, watching the screen.

What should happen

  • Once you're within about 1.5 meters for 2 seconds, both phones switch to Found each other and buzz.
  • Write down if it happened too early, too late, or not at all.
5

Locked phone

Full runQuick run: round 1 only15 min

One phone gets locked, and the other stays open. On the phone you'll lock, set Live Activity in the lab panel before each round:

  1. Round 1: Restart at NI start
  2. Round 2: Keep
  3. Round 3: Restart on background
  4. Round 4: None

Do, for each round

  1. Start a new Find, standing 3 meters apart with the distance showing.
  2. On the phone you'll lock, Mark "Test 5 round 1 locking" (or whichever round).
  3. Lock that phone and put it in a pocket for a full minute. While it's locked, the other person walks about 6 meters away and back.
  4. Unlock, Mark "unlocked", then Export log.

Once all four are done, repeat round 1 one more time, swiping home instead of locking.

What should happen

Nothing to watch on screen. The logs show whether the phone kept measuring while it was locked.

6

Starting from a pocket

Full run15 min

Part A

  1. With the distance showing, tap Restart NI in 10 s on one phone, then lock it right away.
  2. Wait 30 seconds, unlock, and Export log.

Part B

  1. Your partner opens the trip and turns on Let the group find me.
  2. They lock their phone, put it in a pocket, and walk at least 150 meters away.
  3. Start a Find on them and walk toward them.

What should happen

  • Within about 60 meters, their phone may start measuring on its own. Write down whether it did.
  • If it didn't, within about 15 seconds they get a notification: "[Your name] is close. Open Homebound to help them find you." Tapping it should bring up the exact distance.
7

How far it reaches

Full runQuick run: steps 1 to 3 only15 min

Do

  1. In an open field or park, start 2 meters apart.
  2. Walk apart slowly, watching the distance. Write down the distance when Precise goes away.
  3. Walk back and write down when it comes back.
  4. Turn off Allow EDM in the lab panel on both phones and do it again.
  5. Then one short try each: a busy indoor spot, one phone in a pocket, and one phone in a bag.

Write down

For each try, the distance where Precise dropped and where it came back.

8

Battery

Full run45 min

Use the same phone for all three rounds, keep the Find screen open, and keep the brightness the same.

Do

  1. 15 minutes with Gate set to off in the lab panel. That's GPS only.
  2. 15 minutes with Gate set to auto.
  3. 15 minutes with Gate on auto and Camera on in the lab panel.

Write down

The battery percentage at the start and end of each round.

9

Lock screen start

Full run5 min

Do

  1. Your partner turns on Let the group find me, then swipes Homebound away to close it.
  2. Start a Find on them.
  3. They tap Stop on their lock screen.

What should happen

  • Their lock screen shows "[Your name] is finding you" with a Stop button, plus a notification.
  • Tapping Stop ends the Find on both phones.
10

Apple Watch

Optional, if a watch is paired5 min

Do

During a Find, open Homebound on the watch.

What should happen

  • The trip screen shows a row like "Finding Jamie". Tapping it opens the arrow.
  • The arrow follows your wrist as you turn, and the taps speed up once you're within 50 meters.
  • With the phone's Find screen open and the distance precise, the watch shows Precise.
  • Stop on the watch ends the Find.

Reading the UWB lab panel

Phase
What Ultra Wideband is doing. "ranging" means it's measuring.
Caps me / peer
What each phone can do. P is exact distance, D is pointing without the camera, C is the camera arrow, and E is extended range.
Config
Whether extended range (EDM) and the camera are in use right now.
Distance and Angle
The raw Ultra Wideband numbers. Angle is positive when your partner is to your right.
Convergence
How the camera arrow is doing. "converged" means it knows the direction.
Mark and Export log
Mark adds your note to the log. Export log shares the log file.
