Testing Find

Find points two people on the same group trip toward each other with an arrow and a distance. Up close, newer iPhones use Ultra Wideband to measure the distance far more precisely than GPS can. These tests check how that holds up in real places, with real phones.

2 iPhones 2 people Full run: about an hour Quick run: about 20 minutes

The UWB lab in the app walks you through each test. Pick the test, tap Start, follow the steps on screen, and tap Stop. The app measures the result, suggests Pass or Fail where there's a right answer, and keeps your notes. This page has the setup and an overview of each test.

What you need

  • Two iPhones with Ultra Wideband: iPhone 11 or newer, except iPhone SE, 16e and 17e.
  • The Homebound TestFlight build on both phones. Hudson sends the invite.
  • A test account for each phone, also from Hudson.
  • Two people, one per phone.
  • An open field or park, and a busy indoor spot for one short test.

Set up each phone

  1. Install Homebound from TestFlight and sign in with your test account.
  2. In Homebound, go to Settings → Developer Tools → Server Environment and pick Dev Render. This signs you out. Sign in again with the same account.
  3. Back in Settings → Developer Tools, turn on Find UWB Lab.
  4. In the iPhone's Settings app, open Homebound. Set Location to While Using the App or Always, and turn on Precise Location. Turn on Notifications and Live Activities.
  5. Close the Find My app if it's open. It can borrow the Ultra Wideband radio.
  6. Hudson adds both accounts to a group trip. Accept the invite. The trip has to be started for Find to work.

Start a Find

  1. On one phone, open the trip and tap Find & Meet Up. Under Find someone, tap Find next to your partner.
  2. On the other phone, allow it when asked.
  3. The first time, each phone asks to find and measure the distance to nearby devices. Tap Allow on both. Later, Precise arrow asks for the camera. Allow that too.
  4. The UWB lab card shows under the arrow.

Using the UWB lab

  1. Tap Tests and pick the test. The numbers match this page.
  2. Tap Start, follow the steps on screen, and tap Stop. Each round sets up what it needs, so you don't have to change any settings.
  3. Read what the app measured, tap Pass, Fail or Skip, and add a note about anything you saw.
  4. Tap Next round or Next test.

Hold both phones upright, with the backs facing each other. The top line of the card says what Ultra Wideband is doing, in plain words. The slider button opens the raw numbers, which you won't need for the tests.

The results and log hold distances, angles, timings and battery level. They don't hold where you are.

The tests

Same model of iPhone (two 17 Pros, say): do the full run, every test below.

Different models (a 17 Pro and a 16, say): do the quick run, just the tests marked Quick run.

1

Quick check

Full runQuick run5 min

Do

  1. Start a new Find, standing 2 to 3 meters apart. The clock starts with the Find.
  2. Tap Start and wait about 10 seconds.
  3. Tap Stop.

What should happen

  • Both phones show P in their capabilities, and Ultra Wideband is measuring within about 15 seconds of the Find starting.
  • If either phone shows a D, that phone can point without the camera. The app records it either way.
2

Which way is right

Full runQuick run5 min

If the app says it's backwards on either phone, stop and tell Hudson before going on. It's a quick fix, and it changes what the other tests mean.

Do

  1. Tap Start, then open Precise arrow and sweep slowly until the arrow shows.
  2. Partner stands 3 m to your right: tap Partner on my right.
  3. Partner stands 3 m to your left: tap Partner on my left.
  4. Tap Stop. Then do it on the other phone.

What should happen

  • A positive angle with your partner on the right, negative on the left. The app checks the sign for you.
3

The camera arrow

Full run10 min

Do

  1. Tap Start, then open Precise arrow. The app times how long the arrow takes.
  2. Partner walks a full circle around you about 5 m out, then stops behind you.
  3. Tap Stop. Note anything odd, like the arrow lagging. The camera pauses itself after 2 minutes to save battery; tap Resume if you need longer.

What should happen

  • An arrow within about 10 seconds of sweeping, and it keeps up as they move.
  • If the camera picture goes black but the arrow still works, write that in the note. That's a backup mode, not a failure. If it says "Precise arrow couldn't start the camera", tap Try again once.
4

Found each other

Full run5 min

Do

  1. Start about 10 meters apart and tap Start.
  2. Walk toward each other.
  3. Tap Stop once both phones say Found each other, or when you're standing together.

What should happen

  • Both phones switch to Found each other within about 1.5 m, and buzz.
5

Locked phone

Full runQuick run: round 1 only15 min

Five rounds. Each one sets up how the lock screen activity behaves, so just follow the round shown in the app. Round 5 swipes home instead of locking.

Do

  1. Start a new Find, 3 m apart. Tap Start on the phone you'll lock.
  2. Lock it and pocket it for a full minute. Partner walks 6 m away and back.
  3. Unlock and tap Stop.

What should happen

  • Nothing to judge. The app records whether the phone kept measuring while it was locked, like "Kept measuring while away: yes, 412 readings".
6

Starting from a pocket

Full run15 min

Do

  1. Part A: with the distance showing, tap Start, tap Restart NI in 10 s, and lock the phone right away. Wait 30 seconds, unlock, and tap Stop.
  2. Part B: on the phone going in the pocket, turn on Let the group find me, tap Start, lock it, and walk 150 m or more away.
  3. The other phone starts a Find on them and walks toward them.
  4. When you meet, unlock and tap Stop.

What should happen

  • Nothing to judge. The app records whether the locked phone started measuring on its own.
  • If it didn't, within about 15 seconds of the other phone getting close, they get a notification: "[Name] is close. Open Homebound to help them find you." Note whether it came.
7

How far it reaches

Full runQuick run: round 1 only15 min

Five rounds: open field with extended range on, open field with it off, a busy indoor spot, one phone in a pocket, and one in a bag. The app switches extended range for you.

Do

  1. Start 2 m apart in the open and tap Start.
  2. Walk apart slowly. Keep going past where Precise drops (it stops at 50 m on purpose) until UWB reached stops going up, or 100 m.
  3. Walk back until it reads again, then tap Stop.

What should happen

  • Nothing to judge. The app records how far Ultra Wideband reaches, where it lost the other phone, and where it picked it up again.
8

Battery

Full run45 min

Do

  1. Keep the Find screen open and the brightness the same each round. Note the exact battery % first.
  2. Tap Start and leave it for 15 minutes.
  3. Tap Stop, then type the exact start and end %. iOS rounds the app's own numbers to 5%.

What should happen

  • Nothing to judge. The app records the battery used each round: GPS only, then Ultra Wideband, then Ultra Wideband with the camera. It sets each round up for you.
9

Lock screen start

Full run5 min

Do

  1. Partner turns on Let the group find me, then swipes Homebound away.
  2. Tap Start, then start a Find on them.
  3. They tap Stop on their lock screen. Tap Stop in the app.

What should happen

  • Their lock screen shows "[Your name] is finding you" with a Stop button, plus a notification.
  • Tapping Stop ends the Find on both phones.
10

Apple Watch

Optional, if a watch is paired5 min

Do

  1. Tap Start, then open Homebound on the watch during the Find.
  2. Check the row on the trip screen, the arrow, the taps under 50 m, and Stop.
  3. Tap Stop.

What should happen

  • The trip screen shows a row like "Finding Jamie". Tapping it opens the arrow.
  • The arrow follows your wrist, and the taps speed up within 50 meters.
  • Stop on the watch ends the Find.

Send your results

  1. In the UWB lab, tap Results. Every round you marked is there, with what the app measured.
  2. Tap Export. It shares a short summary and the full log.
  3. Send both phones' exports to Hudson. AirDrop them, or email them to support@homeboundapp.com.

To paste the results into a message instead, tap Copy summary.
