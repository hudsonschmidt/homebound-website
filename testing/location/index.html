Testing live location

On a trip, Homebound shares where you are, so the people watching can find you if you're overdue. Real trips are hard on that: the phone is locked in a pocket, you stop for a long break, or iOS closes Homebound. These tests check how your iPhone handles each of those. You walk, the phone keeps a log, and Hudson reads the logs.

1 iPhone Walking 8 tests About 3 hours in total Split it up however you like

Mark each test Done, Problem or Skipped and add notes as you go. Your answers save on this phone, and the Send your results section at the bottom puts them together for you.

What you need

  • An iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Any model.
  • The Homebound TestFlight build. Hudson sends the invite.
  • Developer access on your Homebound account. Tell Hudson the email you sign in with, and he'll turn it on.
  • Somewhere to walk with mobile signal. Around your neighborhood is perfect.
  • Hudson reachable by text for tests 4, 7 and 8. He sends something to your phone from his computer.

Set up your phone

You only do this once.

  1. Install Homebound from TestFlight and sign in.
  2. In the iPhone's Settings app:
    • General → Background App Refresh: on, and on for Homebound.
    • Battery → Low Power Mode: off. Keep the battery above 30% during tests so it doesn't turn itself on.
    • Apps → Homebound → Location: While Using the App for now, with Precise Location on.
    • Apps → Homebound → Notifications: allowed.
  3. In Homebound, go to Settings → Developer → Live Location Spike. If there's no Developer section, close Homebound and open it again. If it's still missing, text Hudson.
  4. Check the Preflight list at the top. Every line needs a green tick. If one is red:
    • Server: in Homebound, go to Settings → Developer Tools → Server Environment and pick Production. Sign in again if it asks.
    • Active trip, circle share or Find session: end it first.
    • Background App Refresh, Low Power Mode or Reduced accuracy: fix it in the iPhone's Settings app (step 2).
  5. Scroll down to Push, tap Copy token, and text it to Hudson. He needs it for tests 4, 7 and 8.

How every test works

  • Settings: each test says what to pick under Controls. Most use the defaults: Mode live, LiveConfiguration fitness, Upload fixes on, Upload interval 60 s, and both Hold switches on.
  • Start and Stop: tap Start to begin. Each Start makes a new log. Tap Stop at the end. While a test is running, the button says Stop; otherwise it says Start.
  • Markers: when a step says to tap Walking or Stopped, unlock, tap it under Markers, and lock again right away. That's how Hudson finds each part in the log.
  • Hands off: otherwise, keep the phone locked in your pocket and don't open Homebound. Each test says when to.
  • Something unexpected? If you got in a car, took a call or opened Homebound by mistake, type it in the box next to Mark, tap Mark, and add it to your notes below.
  • What the log holds: your location every few seconds during a test, since that's what's being tested, plus battery level and app timings. It stays on your phone until you send it to Hudson. During a test, Homebound also sends your location to its server, as it does on a trip. It isn't shared with anyone.

Part 1: Location set to While Using

Short on time? Tests marked Most important (1, 3 and 5) matter most. Do those first.

Tests 4, 7 and 8 need Hudson at his computer, so plan those with him.

1

Locked walk

Most important40 min

Settings

The defaults: live, fitness, 60 s.

Do

  1. Tap Start, then Walking.
  2. Lock the phone, put it in your pocket, and walk for 20 minutes.
  3. Unlock, tap Stopped, and lock again. Stay in one place for 15 minutes. Sitting on a bench is perfect.
  4. Unlock, tap Walking, lock again, and walk for 5 more minutes.
  5. Tap Stop.
2

Older method

20 min

Settings

Mode legacyBG and Upload interval 30 s. Leave the rest.

Do

  1. Tap Start, then Walking.
  2. Lock the phone, put it in your pocket, and walk for 20 minutes.
  3. Tap Stop.
  4. Set Mode back to live and Upload interval back to 60 s, ready for the next tests.
3

Closed by iOS

Most important15 min

Settings

The defaults: live, fitness, 60 s.

Do

  1. Tap Start, then Walking.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Crash now, then Crash now again to confirm. Homebound closes. That's the point: it acts as if iOS shut it down.
  3. Don't open Homebound. Lock the phone and walk for 10 minutes.
  4. Open Homebound, go back to Settings → Developer → Live Location Spike, and tap Stop.

What should happen

Nothing to watch. The log shows whether Homebound came back by itself while you walked.

4

Started by Hudson

Needs Hudson10 min

Settings

None. Don't tap Start for this one.

Do

  1. Make sure the screen shows Start, meaning no test is running.
  2. Swipe up to go to the Home Screen. Don't swipe Homebound away.
  3. Text Hudson "Ready for test 4".
  4. When he says he's sent it, lock the phone and walk around for 5 minutes.
  5. Open Homebound and go to Live Location Spike. If it shows Stop, tap it. If it shows Start, leave it.

Write down

Whether it showed Stop or Start when you opened it.

Part 2: Location set to Always

Before test 5, go to the iPhone's Settings app, then Apps → Homebound → Location, and pick Always. Keep Precise Location on. The first Preflight line should now say Location: Always · Full accuracy.

6

Swiped away

15 min

Settings

The defaults: live, fitness, 60 s.

Do

  1. Tap Start, then Walking.
  2. Open the app switcher by swiping up from the bottom and pausing, then swipe Homebound away.

Write down

Whether a notification saying "Spike: Homebound was closed while sharing" showed up after you swiped it away: yes or no.

7

Quiet pings

Needs Hudson45 min

Every 3 minutes, Hudson sends your phone a silent ping, and the log records how quickly each one arrives. You won't see or hear them.

Settings

The defaults: live, fitness, 60 s.

Do

  1. Tap Start, then Walking. Lock the phone.
  2. Text Hudson "Ready for test 7". When he replies "go", note the time. The steps below use 2:00 as the example.
  3. Walk until 2:16.
  4. At 2:16, unlock, tap Stopped, and lock again. Stay in one place until 2:36.
  5. At 2:36, open the app switcher and swipe Homebound away. Leave the phone locked.
  6. At 2:45, open Homebound, go to Live Location Spike, and tap Stop.

A minute either way is fine. The lock screen shows the time, so there's no need to unlock to check it.
