Group trip art brief

Homebound is a free iPhone app for people heading outside. You plan a trip, choose who should know, and they hear from Homebound if you aren't back on time. Friends can also plan a trip together as a group.

I'm looking for an artist to create a small mountain scene for group trips. It shows on every group trip invitation. This page has everything you need to make it.

What it looks like today

Group trips get a small banner of their own. It shows at the top of a group trip invitation, on the crew step when someone plans a group trip, and as a small square tile when someone opens a link to join a trip. On top of it sits the trip's activity, shown as an emoji on a small rounded tile.

It doesn't change with the time of day or the weather, and nothing on it moves. It needs two versions: light for phones in Light Mode, and dark for Dark Mode. Today the app draws it out of simple flat shapes: the light one is a pale morning and the dark one is dusk. Your art replaces it.

Size and layout

iPhones use 3 pixels per point, so design at 3×.

  • Wide banner: canvas 1320 × 300 px (440 × 100 points). On cards it shows 88 to 96 points tall across a card 343 to 408 points wide, with the top corners rounded (16 points). The image is scaled to fill, so the sides get cut. Keep the main peaks inside the middle 1100 px, between the dashed lines.
  • The activity tile covers the bottom-left corner: a 46 to 52 point tile, 14 points in from the left, half hanging below the banner. Keep the bottom-left 210 × 90 px simple.
  • Square tile: canvas 600 × 600 px. It shows at 56 × 56 points with rounded corners (14 points) and the activity emoji right in the middle, so keep the centre calm. It still has to read as mountains at that small size. A reframe of the wide scene is fine.

Style

Textured flat, like a national park poster. Layered flat shapes, soft gradients in the sky, a fine grain and a little hand-made texture, and a limited palette. Depth comes from ridges that get lighter and bluer with distance. It should be richer than what's there today, but still simple enough to read at a glance at this small size.

Generic Mountain West. Rocky peaks, conifers, perhaps some aspens and a valley floor. It should feel like the Rockies or the Wasatch without being anyone's exact skyline.

Keep it quiet. No text or logos. The trip's activity emoji sits on top, so it needs a calm place to land.

Sit well with the brand. The banner should feel at home next to Homebound's colors. The full palette is on the brand colors page.

The files

  • The wide banner as PNG, 1320 × 300 px, sRGB, no transparency: group-light.png and group-dark.png.
  • The square tile as PNG, 600 × 600 px, sRGB, no transparency: group-square-light.png and group-square-dark.png.
  • Your layered source file (Photoshop, Procreate, Illustrator, Affinity or similar), with the sky, each ridge, the trees and texture on separate layers.

How we'll work

  2. One finished banner: light. We put it in the app and check it on real phones before you go further. Date to be agreed
  3. The dark version and both square tiles. Date to be agreed
