Solo hiking

Solo hiking, with someone who knows your plan.

Tell someone where you're going and when you'll be back. If you're not back on time, Homebound sends them your plan and last known location.

How it works

  1. Plan the hike: where you're going, when you start and when you'll be back.
  2. Add your safety contacts. They get your plan by email, with no app needed.
  3. Start the trip and go. Check in along the way if you like.
  4. Tap I'm Safe when you're back.

If you haven't checked out by your return time plus your grace period, your contacts are alerted with your trip plan and last known location. A grace period is extra time before alerts go out: a 5:00 PM return time with 30 minutes of grace means contacts hear at 5:30 PM.

Out of signal

Start your trip before you lose service. Once Homebound has your trip, the alert goes out from our servers at your return time plus grace, whether or not your phone has a signal.

No service at the trailhead? You can plan the trip anyway. It's saved on your phone and sent when you reconnect, and your contacts only hear about it once it sends. For trips into no-service areas, set your return time for when you expect to be back in coverage.

On your wrist

With Apple Watch you can check in, add time, finish the trip or send an SOS without reaching for your phone. When a trip syncs, the watch sets its own alerts for your return time and the end of your grace period, so they tap you even with no signal.

Part of your plan

Homebound makes sure someone knows your plan. Bring the map, layers and gear your route calls for, and a satellite messenger where there's no coverage.

Homebound doesn't contact 911 or search and rescue. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Questions

What if I'm running late?

Add time from the app or your Apple Watch, from 30 minutes up to 4 hours. Your contacts get an update with the new time.

What if I forget to check out?

Check out as soon as you can. Your contacts get an update that you've marked yourself safe.

Does a missed check-in alert anyone?

No. Only a missed return time does.

Do my contacts need the app?

No. They get emails, and a trip page that opens in any browser. See what safety contacts get.
