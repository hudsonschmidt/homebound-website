For safety contacts

Someone added you as their safety contact.

It means they trust you. They're sharing a trip plan with you: where they're going and when they expect to be back. You don't need the app or an account. Everything comes by email.

The emails you may get

Homebound emails come from an @homeboundapp.com address. You may get one when they plan a trip, when it starts, when they check in or add time, and when they finish.

Most emails have a Follow This Trip link. It opens a page in any browser with the trip's status, a countdown to their return time, their last check-in on a map, the trip's latest photo and a timeline. The page refreshes on its own.

The “expected back by” time in the emails is when Homebound will alert you if they haven't checked out.

If they're not back on time

If they haven't marked themselves safe by then, you'll get an email with “URGENT” and their name in the subject. It has their trip plan: the activity, where they started and where they were headed, their notes, and the last location Homebound has for them, if they checked in.

If they were sharing their live location, the Follow This Trip page will also show where they are and their recent trail.

What to do

  1. Try to reach them first. Call or message them, or someone they're with.
  2. Open the Follow This Trip page from the email for their latest check-in or location.
  3. If you can't reach them and you're worried about their safety, call 911 or your local emergency number, and share the trip plan from the email.

A late return can have a simple reason, like no signal or a dead battery. When they mark themselves safe, you'll get an email saying so.

What Homebound doesn't do

Homebound is not an emergency service. It alerts the people they chose, including you. It doesn't contact 911, search and rescue, or any public emergency service, and delivery depends on connectivity.

Questions

Do I need to download Homebound?

No. Safety contacts get everything by email, and the trip page opens in any browser. If you'd like to follow their trips in the app too, you can get Homebound and add them as a friend.

They missed a check-in. Will I get an alert?

No. A missed check-in on its own doesn't send an alert. Homebound alerts you only if they haven't checked out by the time in the emails.

Can I see where they are right now?

The trip page shows their last check-in, or their destination if they haven't checked in yet. If the trip goes overdue while they're sharing live location, it also shows their live position.

Does Homebound call 911 for them?

No. Homebound alerts their safety contacts only. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Can I stop these emails?

They come because they listed you on a trip. If you'd rather not be their safety contact, tell them and they can remove you. You can also write to support@homeboundapp.com.
