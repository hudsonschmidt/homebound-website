Your day, run by run

Start a ski or snowboard trip and Homebound will count your runs, vertical and top speed as you go, then save the whole day, run by run. It will work on your iPhone on its own, and on your Apple Watch if you wear one.

Someone at home knows the plan

A ski day is a Homebound trip like any other. You set when you'll be off the mountain, add your safety contacts, and head up. They get your plan by email, with no app needed, and they hear from Homebound when you finish.

If you're not back by your return time plus your grace period, they're alerted with your plan and last known location. That part works today, on every trip. Here's what your contacts get.

Help on the mountain

If you need help, one tap will bring up where you are on the mountain, ready to show ski patrol.

Homebound doesn't contact ski patrol, 911 or any emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Your season

History will show your season: days, runs, vertical, top speed and resorts, with your best days and all-time totals. You'll be able to bring in past days from other ski apps or a GPX file in Settings.

Friends on the mountain

Friends who share their location with you will show up on your live ski card when they're at the same resort. You'll also be able to join the friends leaderboard to compare vertical, this week and this season, with friends who join too.

Questions

When does ski tracking come out?

December 16, 2026, in an update to Homebound. If you already have the app, it arrives as an update.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Ski tracking will work on your iPhone on its own. If you have an Apple Watch, you'll see it there too.

Will my safety contacts see my runs?

No. Your safety contacts get your trip plan and the updates about it, not your ski stats. Your runs stay in your History unless you choose to share them.

Does Homebound replace ski patrol or backcountry gear?

No. Homebound keeps the people at home in the loop. It doesn't contact ski patrol or emergency services. Out of bounds, carry the gear and training your route calls for.
