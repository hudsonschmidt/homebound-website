The short version

Homebound is a free safety app for iPhone and Apple Watch: you set a return time and pick your safety contacts before you head out. If you're not back by then, plus your grace period, they get your trip plan and last known location. Version 1.6, out December 16, adds ski and snowboard tracking, with runs, vertical, and top speed on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Fact sheet

Launch
Version 1.6, December 16, 2026
Platforms
iPhone with iOS 26 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later
Price

Free on the App Store, with an optional Homebound+ subscription.

Homebound+ adds resort trail maps with run and lift names, ski day replays, bigger trip photo albums, more safety contacts per trip, and unlimited trip history. Trips, alerts, SOS, and ski tracking are free.

Homebound is not an emergency service. It alerts the people you choose.

What's new in 1.6

  • Start a ski or snowboard trip, and Homebound counts your runs, vertical, and top speed as you go, on your iPhone and Apple Watch, then saves the whole day, run by run.
  • History shows your season, with your best days and all-time totals. With Homebound+, the resort's trails show on your ski maps, and you can replay your day.
  • Friends who share their location with you show up on your live ski card when they're at the same resort.
  • Circles let family and friends see each other on the map. Joining is always a choice, and anyone can pause or leave at any time.
  • Turn on Record my route, and Homebound keeps your trip's path, with distance, elevation, and moving time. Only you see it unless you post it.
  • Homebound has a refreshed look, with new colors in Light and Dark Mode and a sky on Home that follows the time of day.

The story

Hudson Schmidt grew up in Park City, Utah. He built Homebound after a five-day backpacking trip in Idaho's Sawtooth Mountains, out of cell service, while his mom worried back home.

Homebound is built for everyone who loves being outside, and for the people waiting for them to come home.
