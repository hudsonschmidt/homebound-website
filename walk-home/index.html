Walking home

Let someone know you got home.

For the walk home after a late class, a night out or a first date. Start it in one tap, and tap I'm Safe when you're in.

No account needed for your contacts.

One tap, or ask Siri

Save your walk home as a Quick Trip. It remembers where you're going, how long it takes, your grace period, your safety contacts and whether to share your live location, so starting it takes one tap.

If your Quick Trip is called Walk Home, say “Start my walk home in Homebound” and Siri starts it. A Quick Trip heading to a destination can also work out how long the walk takes from a live route.

Share your location on the way

Turn on live location for the trip and the friends watching it can see where you are on the way. It stops when the trip ends.

Circles let you share your location with the friends you choose, whether or not you're on a trip, and you can stop anytime.

Home safe

Tap I'm Safe when you're in, and your contacts get a note that you made it.

If you're not home by your return time plus your grace period, they're alerted with your plan and last known location. If something feels wrong on the way, SOS alerts your contacts right away with your location.

Homebound doesn't contact 911. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

Questions

What if I get home early?

Tap I'm Safe anytime. Your contacts get a note that you're home, and the trip ends.

Why doesn't Siri see my Quick Trip?

Siri can only start Quick Trips that already have a safety contact or friend saved on them, since there's no step in a voice start for choosing who's watching.

Does SOS call 911?

No. SOS alerts your safety contacts only. In immediate danger, call 911 first, then use SOS to loop in your people.

Do my contacts need the app?

No. They get emails, and a trip page that opens in any browser. See what safety contacts get.
